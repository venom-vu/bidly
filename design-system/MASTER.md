## 1. Product & Domain Context
- **Product Name**: Bidly Preconstruction CRM
- **Target Audience**: General Contractors (EPC, MEP, Steel Structure) in Vietnam
- **Key Characteristics**: Multi-billion VND pursuits, 9 fixed pipeline stages S0–S8, server-enforced hard gates, quantitative Go/No-Go scorecards, tender workspaces.

## 4. Layout, Spacing & Container Hierarchy
- **Container**: `max-w-7xl` (`1280px`) with responsive gutters (`px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`).
- **Section Spacing**: `py-16 md:py-24` with alternating subtle backgrounds (`bg-background` / `sf-gradient-bg`).
- **Card Spacing**: `p-6 sm:p-8` with inner content rhythm of `space-y-4` or `space-y-6`.
- **Rhythm Gaps**: `gap-2` (8px), `gap-3` (12px), `gap-4` (16px), `gap-6` (24px), `gap-8` (32px).

## 5. Border Radius & Elevation Hierarchy
- **Radii Scale**:
  - `rounded-full` (9999px): Pill Badges, Status Chips, Search Capsules.
  - `rounded-3xl` (24px): Large Feature Containers, Z-Pattern Banners.

## 6. Component Patterns (Salesforce Style)
- **Top Accent Card**: Clean card with a 6-8px top colored accent bar classifying the product category or stage.
- **High Affordance Inputs**: 40-44px height, light background, crisp border, explicit top label, active ring focus.
- **3-Layer Modal**: Fixed Header with Title & Close button -> Scrollable body with `max-h-[calc(100dvh-8rem)]` and `min-h-0` -> Fixed Footer with Cancel & Submit buttons.
- **Salesforce Mega Menu**: Multi-column dropdown with category headers, short descriptions, and accent badges.
